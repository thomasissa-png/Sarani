## Segment cible

**Primary segment:** Heads of Marketing / Marketing Directors at enterprise consumer electronics, audio, or consumer goods brands who manage retail co-op activations with distribution partners (fnac, Darty, Boulanger, MediaMarkt, etc.). Sophie profile. Has a seasonal brief coming up or has recently struggled with an agency's revision cycles on a co-branded project.

**Secondary segment:** Brand managers at mid-to-large retail groups (Boulanger profile) who need to onboard brand partners' creative agencies quickly for seasonal pushes.

## Email body

---

**Subject:** Two brand guidelines, one brief, fixed price

---

Managing a co-branded retail activation means satisfying two sets of brand guidelines simultaneously — and usually arguing with an agency about scope halfway through.

Bose had the same situation. A seasonal push inside Boulanger's ecosystem. Two distinct visual identities that couldn't dilute each other. A fixed seasonal window.

We delivered [X] campaign formats in [X] days for €1,080 — agreed upfront, invoiced as quoted. No revision disputes. Campaign went live on time.

If you have a retail activation coming up — co-branded or not — we can quote it in 24 hours.

## Notes d'automatisation

Cette séquence s'intègre dans un pipeline nurturing déclenché par :

- **Trigger A (comportemental):** prospect a visité la page `/work` ou `/pricing` sans convertir → J+2, envoyer cet email.
- **Trigger B (sectoriel):** prospect identifié dans le secteur consumer electronics, audio, ou retail grand public → inclure dans la séquence nurturing "retail & seasonal activations" → J+5 après premier contact.
- **Trigger C (manuel):** Thomas identifie un prospect pertinent après un échange LinkedIn ou une intro → envoyer manuellement, personnaliser la ligne d'objet avec le nom de la marque du prospect.

**Personnalisation IA recommandée :** remplacer "retail activation" par le secteur précis du prospect si disponible dans le CRM (ex : "votre prochain push e-commerce" pour un pure player, "votre prochaine activation en point de vente" pour une marque brick-and-mortar).
