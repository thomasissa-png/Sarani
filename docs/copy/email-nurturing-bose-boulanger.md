## Segment cible

**Primary:** Heads of Marketing / Marketing Directors at enterprise consumer electronics, audio, or consumer goods brands who manage retail co-op activations with distribution partners (fnac, Darty, Boulanger, MediaMarkt, etc.). Sophie profile. Has a seasonal brief coming up or has recently run into revision cycles and budget surprises on a co-branded project.

**Secondary:** Brand managers at mid-to-large retail groups who need to onboard brand partners' creative agencies quickly for seasonal pushes.

---

## Subject lines (A/B — choose one)

**A — constraint-first (recommended for cold nurturing):**
> Two brand guidelines, one brief, fixed price

**B — result-first (recommended for warm nurturing — prospect has visited the site or been referred):**
> Bose × Boulanger: [X] formats, €1,080, zero revision disputes

*Why A for cold: it names the exact pain (two guidelines) without giving away the punchline. Sophie opens because she recognises the situation, not because she's being sold to. B works when she already knows Sarani — lead with the proof.*

Bose had the same situation. A "Les Beaux Jours" push inside Boulanger's retail ecosystem. Two distinct identities — both had to hold.

We delivered [X] campaign formats in [X] days for €1,080 — agreed before we started, invoiced as quoted. Approved on first submission. Campaign went live on time.

If you have a seasonal activation coming up, we can quote it in 24 hours.

## Notes d'automatisation

Séquence intégrée dans un pipeline nurturing déclenché par :

- **Trigger A (comportemental):** prospect a visité `/work` ou `/pricing` sans convertir → J+2, envoyer cet email.
- **Trigger B (sectoriel):** prospect identifié dans le secteur consumer electronics, audio, ou retail grand public → inclure dans la séquence nurturing "retail & seasonal activations" → J+5 après premier contact.
- **Trigger C (manuel):** Thomas identifie un prospect pertinent après un échange LinkedIn ou une intro → envoyer manuellement, personnaliser la ligne d'objet avec le nom de la marque si connue.

**Personnalisation IA recommandée :** remplacer "seasonal activation" par le secteur précis du prospect si disponible dans le CRM (ex : "votre prochain push e-commerce" pour un pure player, "votre prochaine campagne point de vente" pour une marque brick-and-mortar).
