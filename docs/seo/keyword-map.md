## Data Methodology — Transparency Notice

Keyword search volumes and difficulty scores listed below are **estimates derived from qualitative SERP analysis and competitive intelligence**, not from Ahrefs or SEMrush paid tools (unavailable in this environment). Where exact figures could not be confirmed, entries are marked **[HYPOTHESE]**. Volume ranges use the following scale based on industry norms and SERP observation:

- **High**: likely >5,000 searches/month (global, English)
- **Medium**: likely 500–5,000 searches/month
- **Low**: likely 50–500 searches/month
- **Niche**: likely <50 searches/month, but high commercial intent

Difficulty is estimated on a 1–100 scale (Ahrefs convention):
- **High (60–100)**: dominant brands (traditional network agencies, incumbent content platforms) hold top positions with strong backlink profiles
- **Medium (30–60)**: mix of established and emerging players, rankable with content + authority
- **Low (1–30)**: long-tail, rankable with a single well-structured article

WebSearch confirmed: the enterprise creative and design outsourcing space is dominated by generic informational content and traditional agency brand pages. Keywords combining "enterprise" with differentiators (speed, fixed pricing, no retainer) are significantly underserved — representing Sarani's primary opportunity for rapid ranking.

## 3. Competitive Gap Analysis — Sarani vs Traditional Agencies

### Keywords traditional network agencies dominate (do NOT contest on homepage — flank via blog)

Based on WebSearch SERP observation, traditional network agency brand pages and large directory sites rank for:
- "creative agency" (broad, very high difficulty — KD >80)
- "enterprise creative" (broad, high difficulty — dominated by large agency brand pages)
- "content creation agency" (broad, high difficulty — directory sites + large platforms)
- "marketing design services" (high difficulty — established agency pages)
- "design services enterprise" (medium-high difficulty — mix of large platforms and agencies)

**Sarani strategy:** Do not attempt to outrank established agency brand pages on these broad keywords in the short term (0–12 months from zero domain authority). Sarani has no domain authority yet. Instead: publish leadership content on differentiator topics (fixed pricing, D+1 delivery, no retainer), own the combination keywords (enterprise + speed + fixed price), and build topical authority through the blog before contesting broader terms.

### Keywords with open space for Sarani (rankable within 12 months)

| Keyword | Why it is open | Sarani angle |

| creative agency no retainer | No current player authentically targets this — it contradicts subscription/retainer models | Pricing page + article |

### Keywords to avoid (wrong persona territory)

- "unlimited graphic design" (SMB positioning — low relevance for Sarani)
- "graphic design subscription cheap" (price-first, wrong signal for enterprise)
- "best graphic design service for small business" (wrong persona)

**Sarani strategy:** Explicitly avoid these keywords — even if rankable, the traffic converts poorly (SMB visitors, not enterprise procurement). Ranking here would attract the wrong leads and dilute the enterprise positioning.
