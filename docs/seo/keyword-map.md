## Data Methodology — Transparency Notice

Keyword search volumes and difficulty scores listed below are **estimates derived from qualitative SERP analysis and competitive intelligence**, not from Ahrefs or SEMrush paid tools (unavailable in this environment). Where exact figures could not be confirmed, entries are marked **[HYPOTHESE]**. Volume ranges use the following scale based on industry norms and SERP observation:

- **High**: likely >5,000 searches/month (global, English)
- **Medium**: likely 500–5,000 searches/month
- **Low**: likely 50–500 searches/month
- **Niche**: likely <50 searches/month, but high commercial intent

Difficulty is estimated on a 1–100 scale (Ahrefs convention):
- **High (60–100)**: dominant brands (Superside, Publicis, WPP) hold top positions with strong backlink profiles
- **Medium (30–60)**: mix of established and emerging players, rankable with content + authority
- **Low (1–30)**: long-tail, rankable with a single well-structured article

WebSearch confirmed: Superside actively targets "creative agency," "enterprise creative agency," "content creation agency," "outsource creative," and "design outsourcing" through a high-volume blog strategy. These are the primary competitive keywords to either contest or flank.

## 3. Competitive Gap Analysis — Sarani vs Superside vs Design Pickle

### Keywords Superside dominates (do NOT contest on homepage — flank via blog)

Based on WebSearch SERP observation, Superside ranks for:
- "creative agency" (broad, very high difficulty — > KD 80)
- "enterprise creative" (broad, high difficulty)
- "content creation agency" (broad, high difficulty — Superside blog + domain authority)
- "unlimited design service" (high difficulty — Superside + Design Pickle + ManyPixels)
- "marketing design services" (high difficulty)

**Sarani strategy:** Do not attempt to outrank Superside on these broad keywords in the short term (0–12 months from zero). Sarani has no domain authority yet. Instead: publish comparison content ("Superside alternative"), own the differentiator keywords (fixed pricing, no retainer, D+1), and build topical authority through the blog before contesting broader terms.

### Keywords Design Pickle / ManyPixels dominate (avoid SMB territory)

- "unlimited graphic design" (SMB positioning — low relevance for Sarani)

- "best graphic design service for small business" (wrong persona)

**Sarani strategy:** Explicitly avoid these keywords — even if rankable, the traffic converts poorly (SMB visitors, not enterprise procurement). Ranking here would attract the wrong leads and dilute the enterprise positioning.
