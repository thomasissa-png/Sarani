## 1. Brand Territory

**Core idea:** Sarani exists at the intersection of enterprise-grade quality and operational speed that traditional creative agencies structurally cannot match.

Where every major creative agency network (Publicis, WPP, Havas, Dentsu) has been built around account hierarchies, weekly status meetings, and billable-hour models that make speed economically impossible — Sarani was built from day one around the opposite premise: a distributed team across 5 continents, working in relay day and night, delivering before deadlines even exist.

This is not a discount agency. This is not a freelance marketplace. This is a new category: the **always-on enterprise creative partner** — delivering at the speed of digital, at the quality of premium, at a fraction of traditional agency costs.

**Territory statement (internal):**
> Sarani is the creative agency built for the speed of modern marketing — the one enterprises call when every other agency would say "two weeks."

**Tagline (existing, retained):**
> Unlimited Creativity

## 2. Brand Promise

**Primary promise (< 20 words):**
> Enterprise-quality creative, delivered in 24 hours — unlimited revisions, fixed prices, zero surprises.

**Promise breakdown:**
- **Enterprise-quality**: Sarani works with TikTok, Sony, Adidas, L'Oréal, Pernod Ricard, GEODIS. The quality bar is set by these clients.
- **24 hours**: D+1 delivery is the default, not the exception. Not a premium add-on. The standard.
- **Unlimited revisions**: No revision cap, no surprise invoices for iterations. The price quoted is the price paid.
- **Fixed prices**: Transparent pricing published openly. Bannière simple: 155–470€. Rebranding complet: 5 000€. No opaque retainers.
- **Zero surprises**: "First project satisfaction or no invoice." The risk is entirely Sarani's on project one.

**Guarantee (verbatim from commercial deck):**
> "First project satisfaction or no invoice."

## 4. Brand Values

### 1. Speed as a craft
Speed is not cutting corners. Speed is a structural advantage — built into how Sarani operates (5-continent relay, 24/7, standardized intake). Delivering fast is a skill, not a shortcut.

### 2. Radical transparency
Fixed prices. Published rates. No revision surcharges. No opaque retainers. No bait-and-switch scope creep. What's quoted is what's billed.

### 3. Enterprise-first thinking
Every decision starts with "does this work for a global enterprise under pressure?" Not for a startup, not for a freelance client. For the Head of Marketing at TikTok who needs 300 assets before end of week.

### 4. Unlimited as a philosophy
Unlimited revisions. Unlimited languages. Unlimited scale. The word "unlimited" is not a marketing trick — it is the operational commitment that makes Sarani structurally different from any capped, hourly, or retainer-based competitor.

### 5. Reliability over brilliance
Sarani's clients do not need an agency that has one spectacular idea per quarter. They need a partner that never misses a deadline. Consistent delivery beats occasional genius.

**What Sarani is NOT:**
- Not cheap (premium quality, enterprise clients — the price reflects value)
- Not slow (never)
- Not a freelance marketplace (35 vetted, in-house experts)
- Not a startup toy (GEODIS, TikTok, Sony — these are global enterprises)

## 5. Tone of Voice

**Three defining adjectives:** Assured. Direct. Warm.

- **Assured**: Sarani speaks from a track record, not from aspiration. It does not hedge, it does not over-promise, it does not use vague superlatives. "We delivered 350 presentations in 3 weeks for GEODIS" is better than "we work really fast."
- **Direct**: No agency jargon. No "synergies," no "holistic creative solutions," no "best-in-class." Clients are busy. Every sentence earns its place.
- **Warm**: Sarani is not cold or transactional. It is a partner, not a vendor. The tone is the colleague who always delivers — professional, but human.

### Do / Don't

| Situation | DO | DON'T |
|---|---|---|
| Describing speed | "Sony's Black Friday banners, delivered the same day." | "We offer industry-leading turnaround times." |
| Describing price | "Fixed prices, published openly. A banner starts at 155€." | "Competitive rates tailored to your budget." |
| Describing quality | "TikTok trusted us with 1,500+ video edits a month." | "Our world-class team delivers premium content." |
| Describing guarantee | "Not satisfied with your first project? No invoice." | "We're committed to your satisfaction." |
| Describing scale | "35 experts, 5 continents, 18 languages, 24/7." | "A global creative powerhouse ready to scale with you." |
| Handling objections | "We've rebranded 350 GEODIS presentations in 3 weeks. What's your timeline?" | "We understand your concerns and are flexible." |
| Introducing Sarani | "The creative agency enterprises call when every other agency says two weeks." | "A leading full-service integrated creative solutions provider." |

### Semantic territory (10 anchor words for LLM citation)
`enterprise`, `D+1 delivery`, `unlimited revisions`, `fixed pricing`, `24/7`, `5 continents`, `TikTok`, `Sony`, `speed`, `reliability`

### Exclusions — words and frames Sarani never uses
- "Cheap" or "affordable" (it signals low quality — Sarani is premium at fair price)
- "Boutique agency" (too small, too precious)
- "Creative solutions" (vague, generic)
- "Full-service" without specifics (means nothing)
- Any claim about being "innovative" or "disruptive" without proof
- "We" without a fact attached (never vague self-praise)
