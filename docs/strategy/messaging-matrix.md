## How to read this document

Each cell contains five fields:
- **Message:** 1 sentence in the persona's own words — not the marketer's words
- **Proof:** Named client, real number, verifiable result
- **Objection:** The real objection at this funnel stage (not the polite version)
- **Vocabulary:** Use / Avoid (aligned with brand-voice.md)
- **CTA:** Specific action verb — never "Learn more"

**Personas:** Sophie (Head of Marketing, enterprise >500M€ CA) · Marc (Procurement Director, enterprise >500M€ CA)
**Channels:** Landing page · Email · LinkedIn · Twitter/X · Paid ads
**Funnel stages:** Discovery · Consideration · Decision · Onboarding · Expansion

**Named proof anchors used throughout:** TikTok (1,500+ videos/month), Sony (same-day banners, 155€), GEODIS (5,700 slides, 3 weeks, 8,500€), Air Corsica (29.9% ROI), Adidas (92 assets, marathon booth)
