## 1. Documented Problem

**Sophie's reality — in her words, not ours.**

Sophie is Head of Marketing at a global enterprise. She manages campaigns across 5+ markets simultaneously, reports to a board that wants results yesterday, and relies on agencies to produce the assets that make everything work.

Here is what happens every week:

- She needs 300 video edits for TikTok's next campaign. Her current agency quotes 3 weeks and a budget approval process that takes longer than the deadline.
- Sony needs Black Friday banners before the sale goes live — that means today, not Friday. Her agency needs a new brief, a new scope, and a new invoice.
- GEODIS is rebranding. 350 presentations. The agency quotes 3 months and €80,000. The board has approved 3 weeks and €8,500.
- She sends a revision note on a banner. She gets back a question: "This revision is outside the original scope — we'll need to raise a change request."

**The quantified cost of this problem:**

| Pain | Measurable impact |
|------|------------------|
| Standard agency turnaround: 1–4 weeks | Every missed campaign window = lost media investment ROI |
| Revision billing | Unpredictable budget overruns; Sophie avoids asking for corrections she knows are right |
| Multi-agency patchwork for multilingual campaigns | 3–5 agencies per campaign, 3–5 account managers, 3–5 briefing processes |
| Network agency pricing | Same banner Sarani delivers at 155€ costs 1,500€+ at a large network |
| Subscription lock-in (Superside: $10,000/month minimum) | 12-month commitment required before one asset is produced |

**Sophie does not have a creativity problem. She has a delivery problem.**

She knows what she wants. She has the brief. She has the budget. What she cannot find is an agency that moves at the speed her campaigns require — without punishing her for every change request.

## 5. For Whom — and NOT for Whom

### Ideal client profile

- **Company size:** Enterprise with revenue >500M€
- **Marketing structure:** Dedicated marketing/communication team with recurring creative output needs
- **Campaign pattern:** Ongoing or burst-mode production (not one annual campaign)
- **Geography:** Multi-market, multi-language requirements (3+ markets or languages)
- **Budget range:** 5,000€–5M€/year in creative production
- **Pain:** Frustrated by agency speed, revision billing, or multi-agency complexity
- **Representative persona:** Sophie (Head of Marketing / Directrice Communication), Marc (Procurement Director / Directeur Achats)

### NOT for Sarani

- **Early-stage startups** that need strategic brand definition before execution — Sarani executes, it does not replace a founding brand strategist
- **One-shot projects under 1,000€** that do not signal a recurring relationship (not a viable account for either party)
- **Companies seeking pure strategic consulting** — Sarani is a creative production and operational marketing agency; it does not replace a McKinsey or a strategy boutique
- **Clients who want the prestige of a big-name agency network** as a risk shield — Sarani's value is speed and precision, not brand-name reassurance for a nervous board
- **Clients unwilling to provide a clear brief** — Sarani's D+1 delivery depends on a clean, specific brief; it is not a discovery agency

## 6. Why Now

### The market is moving faster than agencies can adapt

Content velocity has become the primary competitive advantage in digital marketing. In 2026, TikTok's algorithm rewards daily posting. Meta campaigns require weekly creative refresh to avoid fatigue. LinkedIn B2B content has a 48-hour relevance window.

**Traditional agencies were not built for this.** Their operating model (account hierarchies, weekly WIPs, revision billing, single-timezone teams) makes speed structurally impossible — not a service failure, a structural one.

### The cost of inaction for Sophie

Every week without a reliable, fast creative partner means:
- Campaign assets not refreshed = paid media budget delivering declining ROI
- Multilingual markets underserved = revenue opportunities left on the table
- Revision cycles adding days to launch timelines = competitors in market first
- Budget overruns from surprise revision invoices = procurement scrutiny of agency relationships

### The structural shift that makes Sarani's model win

The combination of distributed remote talent (5 continents), communication infrastructure (Slack, async workflows), and a new generation of enterprise buyers who evaluate vendors on output speed rather than office location — has created a window that closes as soon as a well-funded competitor occupies the space Sarani holds today.

**Sarani has 5 years of enterprise delivery proof (TikTok, Sony, Adidas) and no subscription lock-in.** The window is open now.
