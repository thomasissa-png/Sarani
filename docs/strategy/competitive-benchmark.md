## 1. Benchmark Scope & Methodology

**Market analyzed:** International creative services agencies targeting enterprise clients (Fortune 500 / CAC 40 / global enterprises with revenue >500M€)

**Competitors benchmarked:**
1. **Superside** — premium enterprise creative platform (US/Global)
2. **Design Pickle** — unlimited design subscription (US-centric, SMB to mid-market)
3. **ManyPixels** — unlimited graphic design subscription (Global, SMB to enterprise)
4. **Penji** — unlimited graphic design subscription (US-centric, SMB to mid-market)
5. **Network agencies** (WPP, Publicis, Havas, Dentsu) — traditional enterprise incumbents

**Methodology:** Public website analysis + pricing pages + client testimonials + review aggregators (G2, Endorsal, SourceForge). Data collected via WebSearch 2026-03-24. No primary interviews conducted (not available at this stage).

**Key question this benchmark answers:**
> What is every major competitor doing — and what space is demonstrably unoccupied that Sarani can own?

## 2. Competitor Profiles

### 2.1 Superside
**Website:** superside.com
**Tagline:** "Your creative team's creative team"
**Positioning:** Premium enterprise design platform. Scalable, subscription-based creative partner for mid-market and enterprise brands.
**Pricing:** Starts at $10,000/month (subscription). Typical range: $10K–$100K/month. Fixed monthly service fee of $1,000 + variable budget.
**Target client:** Enterprise / mid-market (Amazon, Google, Meta, Salesforce, Cisco, Coinbase, Notion)
**Key strengths:**
- Strong enterprise client roster
- AI-enhanced workflows (custom brand-trained image models)
- Forrester-verified: 94% 3-year ROI, 6-month payback
- 4.6/5 on Endorsal (467+ reviews, 91% recommend)
**Key weaknesses:**
- $10,000/month minimum creates high barrier — no project-by-project option
- Design-focused: not full-service (limited strategy, copy, video, paid ads)
- No satisfaction guarantee documented publicly
- Subscription lock-in model — clients must commit upfront
- No per-project transparent pricing
**What Superside does that Sarani should NOT replicate:**
- Subscription-only model that excludes clients who need occasional bursts
- Positioning as a "team extension" (implies long-term, slow onboarding)

---

### 2.2 Design Pickle
**Website:** designpickle.com
**Positioning:** Affordable unlimited graphic design subscription. Originally the pioneer of the subscription design model.
**Pricing (2025 post-refresh):** ~$1,918/month for 2 creative hours/day (base platform $119 + creative hours add-on). Enterprise/Power Plans: custom pricing on request.
**Target client:** SMB to mid-market. Agencies reselling design services.
**Key strengths:**
- Pioneer brand recognition in subscription design
- Flexible hourly model (scale hours up/down)
- AI tools integrated into platform (Stockpress)
- Unlimited revisions included
**Key weaknesses:**
- SMB/mid-market positioning — not credible for enterprise like TikTok or Sony
- Output capped by daily hours (queue model — one request at a time)
- Hourly model means volume clients need expensive scaling
- US-centric team — limited multilingual capability
- Does not offer strategy, video production, paid ads management
**What Design Pickle does that Sarani should NOT replicate:**
- Presenting the business primarily as "cost-saving" (invites race-to-bottom perception)
- Queue/hourly model (creates bottlenecks at the moment enterprise clients need scale)

---

### 2.3 ManyPixels
**Website:** manypixels.co
**Positioning:** Best price-to-quality ratio in unlimited design. Subscription service for businesses wanting reliable design without freelancer risk.
**Pricing (2025):** $599/month (Advanced, 1 output/day) to $1,299/month (Designated Designer). Annual plans from $439/month.
**Target client:** Small businesses, startups, mid-size businesses, agencies. Not enterprise-focused.
**Key strengths:**
- Broad service scope (80+ design categories including web design)
- Dedicated designer option with Slack access
- Strong price-quality reputation
- No contracts (month-to-month)
**Key weaknesses:**
- Not positioned for enterprise — client base is SMB/mid-market
- Output limited to design (no copywriting, no video production, no paid ads)
- Daily output cap (1–2 designs/day) — insufficient for enterprise volume
- No named enterprise clients documented
**What ManyPixels does that Sarani should NOT replicate:**
- "Best value" positioning (positions on price rather than enterprise trust)
- Catering to freelancers and startups alongside enterprise (dilutes credibility)

---

### 2.4 Penji
**Website:** penji.co
**Positioning:** Subscription graphic design service. Cost-effective alternative to hiring an in-house designer or using a traditional agency.
**Pricing (2025):** $499/month (Business, 2 brands) to $1,497/month (Agency, priority support). 15-day money-back guarantee.
**Target client:** SMB, marketing teams, agencies. Named clients: AWeber, Lyft, Reebok, University of Pennsylvania.
**Key strengths:**
- 24–48h turnaround on most requests
- Point-and-click revision tool (UX differentiator for non-designers)
- 15-day money-back guarantee (risk reduction)
- 120+ design service categories
**Key weaknesses:**
- SMB-focused client roster — not enterprise credibility
- US-centric team and workflow
- Design only — no strategy, copy, video, paid ads
- Same-day delivery only on top-tier plan ($1,497/month)
- Not built for volume at TikTok scale (1,500+/month)
**What Penji does that Sarani should NOT replicate:**
- Positioning as an alternative to hiring a freelancer (anchors on small-scale thinking)
- Money-back guarantee framing (weaker signal than Sarani's "no invoice" — implies client pays first, then disputes)

---

### 2.5 Network Agencies (WPP, Publicis, Havas, Dentsu)
**Positioning:** Integrated communication and advertising networks. Prestige, strategic depth, global presence.
**Pricing:** Custom. Typically 50K€–500K€+ annual retainers for large accounts. Hourly rates 150–400€/hour.
**Target client:** Fortune 500 / CAC 40. Same target as Sarani.
**Key strengths:**
- Brand prestige — safe choice for a CMO under scrutiny
- Strategic capabilities (research, planning, media buying at scale)
- Long-standing enterprise relationships
- Compliance and legal infrastructure
**Key weaknesses:**
- **Speed:** 2–4 weeks minimum for standard deliverables. 3+ months for major campaigns.
- **Rigidity:** Each revision = a new quote. Scope creep is institutionalized.
- **Cost:** Order of magnitude more expensive. Same banner Sarani delivers at 155€ costs 1,500€+ at a network agency.
- **Disconnect:** Senior talent pitches the account; junior staff executes.
- **Multilingual:** Requires a local agency per market — fragmented management.
**What network agencies do that Sarani should NOT replicate:**
- Everything. Sarani's existence is predicated on doing the opposite.
- The "discovery phase" before any work starts
- Account team as intermediary between client and creative
- Revision billing

## 3. What Every Competitor Does (patterns to avoid or challenge)

These are patterns shared by ALL competitors in the benchmark. Sarani differentiates by doing the opposite.

| Pattern | All competitors do this | Sarani's counter-position |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription or retainer model** | Superside ($10K/month), Design Pickle ($1,918/month), ManyPixels ($599/month), Penji ($499/month) all require monthly subscriptions | Sarani offers per-project pricing — no forced subscription. Enterprise clients can start with one project. |
| **Speed as a premium add-on** | Same-day or priority delivery only on top-tier/most expensive plans | D+1 is Sarani's standard — for every client, every project |
| **Design only** | Design Pickle, ManyPixels, Penji: design-only. Superside: design-focused. | Sarani: 4 pillars (Strategic Marketing + Content Creation + Operational Marketing + On-demand). Copy, video, paid ads, SEO — all in-house. |
| **English-first / US-centric** | All subscription competitors are primarily English-language, US-timezone | Sarani: 18 languages, 5 continents, 24/7 relay — genuinely international |
| **SMB or mid-market default** | Design Pickle, ManyPixels, Penji: built for SMB. Superside: enterprise but starts at $10K. | Sarani: enterprise by design. Client roster (TikTok, Sony, Adidas) proves it. |
| **No named enterprise clients at SMB tier** | No subscription agency at under $2,000/month has documented Fortune 500/CAC 40 clients | Sarani has TikTok, Sony, L'Oréal, Adidas at project-based pricing |
| **Satisfaction guarantee is a refund window** | Penji: 15-day money-back. Design Pickle: not documented. Superside: not documented. | Sarani: "First project satisfaction or no invoice" — client never pays for a bad first project |
| **Separate agencies per market** | Network agencies manage local agencies per country; subscription tools are single-language | Sarani replaces the multi-agency patchwork with one partner |

## 4. Free Space Map — Differentiation Opportunities

The following spaces are demonstrably unoccupied by any benchmarked competitor:

### Space 1 — Enterprise-grade quality + D+1 delivery + no subscription lock-in
**Who occupies this today:** Nobody.
- Network agencies: enterprise-grade, but 2–4 weeks, no flexibility
- Superside: enterprise-grade, but $10K/month minimum subscription
- Design Pickle/ManyPixels/Penji: fast, but SMB quality, SMB clients
- **Sarani sits in this empty space:** Enterprise client roster (TikTok, Sony) + D+1 standard + per-project pricing

### Space 2 — True multilingual, multi-continent execution (not just "available internationally")
**Who occupies this today:** Nobody below network-agency pricing.
- Network agencies charge enterprise rates for multilingual campaigns
- Subscription services are English-centric
- **Sarani: 18 languages, 5 continents, single point of contact, project pricing**

### Space 3 — Full-service creative (strategy + content + performance) at speed
**Who occupies this today:** Nobody.
- Subscription services are design-only
- Network agencies are full-service but slow and expensive
- **Sarani: 4 pillars cover the full marketing production stack**

### Space 4 — Enterprise clients who need short-term bursts (not annual commitments)
**Who occupies this today:** Nobody.
- Superside requires $10K/month subscription — too heavy for a 2-week campaign push
- Freelancers don't have enterprise capacity
- **Sarani: project-by-project, no minimum commitment, starts same week**

### Priority recommendation
**Space 1 is the primary free space to own.** It is the most defensible, directly connected to Sarani's proof points (client roster), and is the exact pain point Sophie experiences every week. Spaces 2, 3, and 4 reinforce and amplify Space 1.
