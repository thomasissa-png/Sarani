## 1. Top 10 Case Studies — Ranked by Impact

Selection criteria applied for Sophie (Head of Marketing, large enterprise):
- Does it prove volume? Does it prove speed? Does it carry a measurable result?
- Is the client name immediately recognisable to a Fortune 500 marketing director?
- Does it show a capability that Sophie personally fears she can't source elsewhere?
- Is it visually spectacular enough to stop the scroll?

---

### #1 — TikTok Gaming Showcase
**Client:** TikTok
**Metric:** 300 million cumulative views — 3 markets — 80,000€
**Category:** Video / Social / Multi-market
**Why it leads:** This is the single most powerful proof of scale and ROI in the entire portfolio. 300M views across 3 countries anchors Sarani's claim to enterprise-grade creative production immediately. The budget figure (80,000€) positions Sarani squarely in the enterprise tier — not cheap, not boutique. For Sophie, this says: "They play at my level."
**Key message:** Scale. Multi-market. Results.

---

### #2 — TikTok #GimmeTheMic Germany
**Client:** TikTok
**Metric:** 94 million views — 3,800€
**Category:** Video / Social
**Why it's #2:** 94 million views for 3,800€ is the most dramatic ROI proof in the deck. This single data point answers Sophie's core question: "Can they deliver results that matter to my board?" The price-to-impact ratio makes it unforgettable in a way that polished agency portfolios never achieve. For procurement Marc: the number speaks before any sales conversation begins.
**Key message:** ROI. Proof. No agency can touch this ratio.

---

### #3 — TikTok Massive UGC Edits
**Client:** TikTok
**Metric:** 1,500+ videos per month — $20 per video
**Category:** Video / Ongoing Operations
**Why it's #3:** Volume at speed is Sarani's operational superpower. 1,500+ edits per month on a retainer basis proves that Sarani is not a project-by-project boutique — it is an operational partner capable of running a programme at the scale of one of the world's largest platforms. Sophie thinks: "If they handle TikTok's production at this volume, they can handle my 20 assets for next week's campaign."
**Key message:** Volume. Reliability. Operational scale.

---

### #4 — TikTok #RoadToParis
**Client:** TikTok
**Metric:** 51 million views — 360€ — 24 revision rounds
**Category:** Video
**Why it's #4:** 51 million views for 360€ is jaw-dropping. But the detail that seals it is the 24 revision rounds at no extra cost — this directly proves the "unlimited revisions, fixed price, no surprises" promise that differentiates Sarani from every agency Sophie has worked with. It is the best single case study to prove the pricing model, not just the results.
**Key message:** Fixed price. Unlimited revisions. Extraordinary results.

---

### #5 — Adidas Superstar Concert (Adidas Arena Paris)
**Client:** Adidas
**Metric:** Stadium transformed into a giant shoebox — hundreds of assets — full venue
**Category:** Event / Graphic Design
**Why it's #5:** This is Sarani's most visually spectacular case study. A stadium turned into a shoebox for a single night communicates creative ambition at a scale that no brief can fully capture. For Sophie, this says: "They can handle anything I throw at them — physically, logistically, creatively." This is the case study that makes senior marketers stop scrolling and ask "wait, they did that?"
**Key message:** Creative ambition. Scale. Full-venue production.

---

### #6 — LEGO Grand Tournoi des Champs-Élysées
**Client:** LEGO
**Metric:** 250+ teams — Champs-Élysées — full campaign + web platform + event assets
**Category:** Event / Graphic Design / Web
**Why it's #6:** The Champs-Élysées is one of the most recognisable addresses on earth. For a marketing director, seeing LEGO's name on Sarani's portfolio next to that address communicates top-tier clientele and top-tier creative capability. The breadth is also exceptional: campaign posters, web platform, on-site scenography, digital content — all under one brief. This proves Sarani's integrated production capability, not just design output.
**Key message:** Prestige. Location. Integrated production.

---

### #7 — Sony TV Launch Europe
**Client:** Sony
**Metric:** 125 assets — 15 languages — 2 weeks — 8,500€ — daily HQ Japan approval
**Category:** Graphic Design / Multilingual
**Why it's #7:** This case study proves the two capabilities that Sophie most struggles to find simultaneously: multilingual production at volume, and a team that can absorb daily review cycles from a demanding international client (HQ Japan approval, overnight). It also directly addresses Marc's concern: 8,500€ for 125 assets across 15 languages is the kind of benchmark that makes procurement question every agency invoice they've signed in the past three years.
**Key message:** Multilingual. Volume. Complex client structure. Transparent pricing.

---

### #8 — IKEA BILLYothèque (Open-air library on the Seine, Paris)
**Client:** IKEA
**Metric:** Open-air library installation on the Seine — Paris landmark activation
**Category:** Event / Graphic Design
**Why it's #8:** After two Sony data-heavy entries, the BILLYothèque brings a different register: cultural credibility. A public installation on the Seine for a global retailer at the heart of Paris Design Week positions Sarani alongside the cultural institutions that the world's most prestigious brands want to be associated with. It also signals that Sarani's work is visible at street level, not just on screens — relevant for any brand running experiential campaigns.
**Key message:** Cultural credibility. Paris landmark. Design leadership.

---

### #9 — Sony Black Friday (Same-Day Delivery)
**Client:** Sony
**Metric:** Banners delivered same day — 150€ — brief to file in under 24 hours
**Category:** Graphic Design / Speed
**Why it's #9:** This is Sarani's most relatable case study for Sophie's daily reality. She has lived this exact scenario: a campaign brief arriving at 9am that needs assets live the next morning. 150€ and a same-day delivery is the most direct proof of the D+1 promise — not abstract, not aspirational, but documented, invoiced, and delivered. It is the case study that closes the "yes but can they really do it that fast?" objection before it is raised.
**Key message:** Speed. D+1. Real-world proof.

---

### #10 — Crocs x TikTok Shop — Times Square NYC
**Client:** Crocs x TikTok
**Metric:** Times Square NYC billboards
**Category:** Event / Out-of-Home
**Why it's #10:** Times Square is the world's most famous advertising location. A case study with Times Square in the headline communicates global reach and cultural ambition in three words. For a Head of Marketing at an international company, seeing this on a creative partner's portfolio is an immediate signal: these people operate at the same altitude as the briefs I write.
**Key message:** Global reach. Premium locations. Iconic activations.
