## 0. How to Use This Brief

This document answers the question every agent, copywriter, designer, and growth specialist should ask before creating anything for Sarani:

> "Does this feel like Sarani — or could it be any agency?"

If the answer is "any agency" — rework it. Every Sarani touchpoint must be:
1. **Fast** — in spirit and in form. Short sentences. Direct claims.
2. **Proven** — anchored to named clients, real numbers, verifiable results.
3. **Enterprise-worthy** — the Head of Marketing at Sony or TikTok is reading this. Would it impress them?

## 4. Tone of Voice (3 adjectives + rules)

**Three adjectives: Assured. Direct. Warm.**

### Assured
Speak from a track record, not from aspiration. Sarani does not claim to be "world-class" — it points to TikTok, Sony, and Adidas and says "see for yourself."
- DO: "Sony trusted us with 125 assets for their TV launch."
- DON'T: "Our world-class creative team delivers exceptional results."

### Direct
No agency jargon. No filler. Clients are busy executives. Every sentence must earn its place.
- DO: "A banner in 24 hours. Starts at 155€."
- DON'T: "We offer competitive, industry-leading turnaround times with flexible pricing options."

### Warm
Sarani is a partner, not a vendor. Not cold, not transactional, not corporate. Think: the colleague who always comes through.
- DO: "We're the team your team calls when everything has to be right by tomorrow."
- DON'T: "As a leading creative solutions provider, we are committed to delivering value."

### Sentence structure rules for all agents
- Maximum sentence length: 20 words for claims, 30 words for explanations
- Lead with the claim, support with the proof
- Active voice, present tense where possible
- Named clients > "leading brands" every time
- Numbers > adjectives every time ("60% savings" > "significant savings")

## 7. Exclusions — What Sarani Never Says or Does

### Words and phrases — never use
| Banned term | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Cheap" or "affordable" | Signals low quality. Sarani is premium-priced for value. | "Fixed pricing," "transparent rates," "up to 60% savings" |
| "Boutique agency" | Implies small, precious, slow | "International creative agency" |
| "World-class" / "best-in-class" | Vague, unverifiable, used by every agency | Cite TikTok, Sony, Adidas instead |
| "Creative solutions" | Agency jargon, meaningless | Describe the actual deliverable |
| "Holistic" / "synergies" | Corporate filler | Delete. Start a new sentence with a fact. |
| "Innovative" (without proof) | Overused, meaningless | "We edited 1,500 TikTok videos in a month." |
| "Passionate about" | Cliché | Show the work |
| "Full-service" (alone) | Means nothing without specifics | List the actual 4 pillars |
| "Disruptive" | Startup cliché | Not appropriate for Sarani's positioning |
| "We" without a fact | Vague self-praise | Always attach a claim to "we" |

### Creative directions — never pursue
- Do not position Sarani as a budget alternative — it is a premium alternative (different axis)
- Do not use stock photography of generic creative teams — use real Sarani work, real client results
- Do not use abstract or purely aesthetic visuals without an associated claim or case study
- Do not create content that speaks to startups, freelancers, or SMBs — it dilutes the enterprise positioning
- Do not promise what is not operationally guaranteed (do not over-promise on speed for complex projects unless confirmed)

### What Sarani is NOT (explicit rejections)
- Not a freelance marketplace
- Not a design subscription service
- Not a cheap alternative
- Not slow
- Not an agency that bills for revisions
- Not a startup (founded 2020, enterprise clients since day one)

## 8. Channel-Specific Creative Guidelines

### Website (primary conversion tool)
**Agent responsible:** @ux, @copywriter, @design
**Primary persona:** Sophie
**Objective:** Convert a skeptical Head of Marketing who has 90 seconds to decide if Sarani is worth a first project
**Rules:**
- Hero message must include D+1 claim and at least one named enterprise client within 3 seconds of page load
- Social proof (TikTok, Sony, Adidas logos or case study snippets) must appear above the fold or within first scroll
- Pricing must be accessible in max 2 clicks — transparency is a brand value, not a page-deep FAQ
- CTA should be friction-free: "Start a project" > "Schedule a demo" > "Contact us" (in order of preference)
- Mobile-first: Sophie checks new vendors on her phone during a commute

### LinkedIn (primary acquisition channel at 500€/month budget)
**Agent responsible:** @social, @copywriter
**Primary persona:** Sophie (direct) + Marc (via Sophie sharing)
**Objective:** Build trust with enterprise marketing leaders; generate inbound referrals; establish Sarani as the go-to answer to "we need this in 24h"
**Rules:**
- Case studies with real numbers perform best (TikTok views, GEODIS slide count, Sony same-day delivery)
- Tone: peer-to-peer, not B2B broadcast. Write as if Sophie is talking to a colleague.
- Never sell directly in a post — lead with the problem or the result, not the pitch
- Post frequency: consistency > volume. One strong post beats five weak ones.

### Commercial deck / proposals (Marc's touchpoint)
**Agent responsible:** @copywriter (with input from project-context pricing data)
**Primary persona:** Marc
**Objective:** Satisfy procurement criteria, justify vendor choice to CFO
**Rules:**
- Lead with client reference logos and named results
- Pricing tables must be clear, no asterisks, no "from" without "to"
- Include the savings comparison explicitly (e.g., "Sony banner: 155€ at Sarani vs. 1,500€+ at network agencies")
- Include the guarantee prominently: "First project satisfaction or no invoice"
- Frame the no-subscription model as a benefit: "No retainer. No minimum commitment. Pay per project."

### SEO / editorial content
**Agent responsible:** @seo, @copywriter
**Objective:** Rank for searches made by Sophie and Marc when they are looking for a fast, international creative agency
**Key search intents to target:**
- "International creative agency fast delivery"
- "Enterprise creative agency 24h turnaround"
- "Creative agency TikTok Sony Adidas" (brand association)
- "Unlimited revisions creative agency enterprise"
- "Replace agency freelancer enterprise marketing"
**Rules:**
- Every blog post / case study must cite at least one named client and one verifiable number
- Case study format preferred: Problem → Sarani approach → Result (quantified)
- Structured data (FAQ schema, Organization schema) should include anchor phrases from Section 5

## 9. Success Metrics for Creative Output

Every piece of Sarani content — a web page, a LinkedIn post, a proposal section — should be evaluated against these criteria before publishing:

| Criterion | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| **Enterprise-worthy** | A Head of Marketing at Sony would not find this patronizing or SMB-targeted | It could be sent to a 5-person startup |
| **Claim is verified** | Contains at least one named client OR one verifiable number | Contains only adjectives ("world-class," "leading," "best") |
| **Speed is present** | D+1, 24h, or same-day appears naturally in the copy | Speed is not mentioned at all |
| **Tone is direct** | No sentence over 30 words. No jargon. | Contains "holistic," "synergies," "solutions," or "passionate about" |
| **Differentiation is clear** | The reader understands what makes Sarani different from Publicis or Superside | The content could apply to any creative agency |
| **CTA is clear** | One unambiguous next step | Multiple CTAs or vague "learn more" without context |
