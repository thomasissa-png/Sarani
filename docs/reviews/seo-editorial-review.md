## Article 4 vs Article 6: Overlap Risk

Articles 4 ("How Much Does a Creative Agency Cost?") and 6 ("Fixed Pricing vs Retainers") cover overlapping territory — both discuss fixed pricing vs retainer models with the same case studies (GEODIS, TikTok). Article 3 ("How Enterprise Teams Scale Creative Production Without Subscription Lock-In") also overlaps with Article 4 on subscription vs fixed pricing.

**Risk:** keyword cannibalization between articles 3, 4, and 6. Each targets a distinct keyword (LT4, LT5, LT3 respectively), which mitigates the risk. But the content overlap means Google may struggle to differentiate their ranking signals.

**Recommendation:** ensure internal links between these three articles are strong (cluster linking per blog-architecture.md Rule 2) so Google treats them as a topical cluster rather than competing pages.
